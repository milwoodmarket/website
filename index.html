Visitor guide

Information

Everyone is welcome at Milwood Market — admission is always free! Leashed pets, strollers, and wagons are welcome. A shaded seating area is available with picnic tables and a small playscape for the littles. We are located in the Milwood Shopping Center parking lot, adjacent to the community garden and Milwood Library. Come ready to enjoy handcrafted goods, fresh food, and more.


Parking

Parking inside the Milwood Shopping Center lot is reserved for tenants and tenant guests only and will be strictly enforced.

Market visitors may park on Adelphi Ln, which is just a short walk from the entrance. Please be mindful of posted parking signs, and do not block bike lanes, driveways, or intersections.
